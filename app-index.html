Cannot make the answer key in this format? Let AI do it in 3 easy steps

  1. TAP the big button "COPY PROMPT" below.
  2. Open ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude or any AI app. ATTACH your answer key (PDF or photo), then PASTE the prompt (long-press and tap Paste) and send it.
  3. The AI will reply with a list. Tap the AI's COPY button, come back here and tap "PASTE INTO ANSWER BOX". DONE!
